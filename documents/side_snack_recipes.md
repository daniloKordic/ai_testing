# Side Dishes & Snack Recipes

## Roasted Rainbow Vegetables

A colorful, nutritious side dish featuring multiple vegetables.

### Ingredients
- 2 medium carrots, cut into batons
- 2 bell peppers (yellow and red), cut into chunks
- 1 small head broccoli, cut into florets
- 8 oz cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Fresh parsley for garnish

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss all vegetables with olive oil, garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper
3. Spread on baking sheet in single layer
4. Roast for 25-30 minutes, stirring halfway through
5. Remove from oven and drizzle with lemon juice
6. Garnish with fresh parsley
7. Serve immediately

### Nutrition Highlights
- Rainbow of colors ensures variety of phytonutrients
- Carrots provide beta-carotene
- Peppers and tomatoes offer vitamin C
- Broccoli provides calcium and vitamin K

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4-6. Perfect alongside grilled fish or poultry.

## Crispy Kale Chips

A healthy snack packed with nutrients and satisfying crunch.

### Ingredients
- 1 large bunch fresh kale
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- Optional seasonings: paprika, nutritional yeast, black pepper

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C)
2. Wash and thoroughly dry kale
3. Remove stems and tear leaves into chip-sized pieces
4. In large bowl, toss kale with olive oil
5. Sprinkle with sea salt, garlic powder, and any optional seasonings
6. Toss to evenly coat
7. Spread on baking sheets in single layer
8. Bake for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway through
9. Chips should be crispy and slightly browned
10. Cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes; they'll continue to crisp
11. Store in airtight container for up to 3 days

### Nutrition Highlights
- Kale is extremely nutrient-dense with vitamins K, A, and C
- Minimal calories with maximum nutrition
- Low fat, high fiber snack
- Great alternative to potato chips

### Flavor Variations
- Italian: Add dried oregano and basil
- Spicy: Add cayenne pepper and cumin
- Asian: Add sesame seeds and soy sauce
- Umami: Use nutritional yeast and garlic powder

## Carrot & Pepper Hummus with Fresh Vegetables

A colorful dip with fresh roasted vegetables.

### Ingredients
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup roasted carrots (about 3 medium, cut in chunks)
- 1 roasted red bell pepper, jarred or fresh
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons tahini
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Water as needed for consistency

### Vegetables for Serving
- Bell pepper strips
- Carrot sticks
- Broccoli florets
- Tomato slices
- Crispy kale chips

### Instructions
1. Add chickpeas, roasted carrots, and roasted pepper to food processor
2. Add garlic, tahini, lemon juice, and olive oil
3. Add cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper
4. Process until smooth, adding water 1 tablespoon at a time until desired consistency
5. Transfer to serving bowl
6. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with paprika
7. Serve with fresh vegetable crudités

### Nutrition Highlights
- Chickpeas provide plant-based protein and fiber
- Carrots offer beta-carotene
- Roasted peppers provide vitamin C and antioxidants
- Tahini provides calcium and healthy fats

### Storage
Keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days. Perfect for meal prep.

## Stuffed Tomato Appetizers

Elegant appetizers celebrating fresh tomatoes and herbs.

### Ingredients
- 6 medium firm tomatoes
- 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped
- 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup pine nuts, lightly toasted
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Balsamic glaze for drizzling

### Instructions
1. Cut tops off tomatoes and scoop out centers with spoon
2. Reserve scooped tomato flesh and finely chop
3. Heat olive oil in skillet over medium heat
4. Sauté garlic for 30 seconds until fragrant
5. Add chopped spinach and cook for 2-3 minutes until wilted
6. Stir in chopped tomato flesh and cook for 1-2 minutes
7. Remove from heat and cool slightly
8. Mix in goat cheese, basil, pine nuts, salt, and pepper
9. Stuff mixture into hollowed tomatoes
10. Serve at room temperature drizzled with balsamic glaze

### Nutrition Highlights
- Tomatoes provide lycopene and vitamin C
- Spinach offers iron and vitamin K
- Goat cheese provides calcium and probiotics
- Pine nuts add healthy fats and minerals

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 6. Perfect as a party appetizer or light lunch.

## Garlic Herb Roasted Carrots

A simple, elegant side dish celebrating carrots.

### Ingredients
- 1.5 lbs carrots, cut into batons
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)
2. In small bowl, combine olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, mustard, and lemon juice
3. Toss carrots with herb mixture
4. Spread on baking sheet and season with salt and pepper
5. Roast for 25-30 minutes, stirring halfway through
6. Carrots should be tender and caramelized at edges
7. Garnish with fresh parsley
8. Serve warm

### Nutrition Highlights
- Carrots provide beta-carotene for eye health
- Roasting concentrates natural sweetness and nutrients
- Herbs provide antioxidants and anti-inflammatory compounds
- Low calorie, high nutrition side dish

## Crispy Baked Broccoli Florets

A healthier alternative to fried broccoli.

### Ingredients
- 1 large head fresh broccoli, cut into florets
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs (or whole wheat)
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss broccoli florets with 1 tablespoon olive oil
3. In shallow bowl, combine panko, Parmesan, garlic, Italian seasoning, paprika, salt, and pepper
4. In another bowl, whisk remaining olive oil with 2 tablespoons water
5. Coat each broccoli floret in oil-water mixture, then dredge in breadcrumb mixture
6. Place on baking sheet lined with parchment paper
7. Bake for 15-20 minutes until crispy and golden brown
8. Serve immediately

### Nutrition Highlights
- Broccoli provides sulforaphane with anti-cancer properties
- Baking instead of frying reduces fat and calories
- Whole wheat breadcrumbs add fiber
- Parmesan adds calcium and flavor

## Strawberry & Arugula Toast

A simple yet elegant breakfast or snack option.

### Ingredients
- 2 slices whole grain bread
- 2 tablespoons cream cheese or goat cheese
- 1 cup fresh strawberries, sliced
- 2 cups fresh arugula
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons sliced almonds
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Toast bread to desired crispness
2. Spread cream cheese or goat cheese on each toast
3. In small bowl, toss arugula with olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper
4. Top each toast with arugula mixture
5. Arrange sliced strawberries on top
6. Drizzle with honey
7. Sprinkle with sliced almonds
8. Serve immediately

### Nutrition Highlights
- Whole grain bread provides fiber and B vitamins
- Strawberries offer vitamin C and antioxidants
- Arugula provides vitamin K and calcium
- Almonds add protein and healthy fats

### Variations
- Use ricotta instead of cream cheese
- Add balsamic reduction instead of drizzle
- Include walnuts or hazelnuts
- Top with pomegranate seeds for extra tartness
