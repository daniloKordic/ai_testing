# Fruit & Vegetable Smoothie Recipes

## Green Power Smoothie

A nutrient-dense smoothie packed with leafy greens and tropical fruits.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 1 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup fresh pineapple chunks (or frozen)
- 1/2 cup coconut milk
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon raw almond butter
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 cup ice cubes

### Instructions
1. Add spinach and liquid (coconut milk) to blender first
2. Add frozen banana, pineapple, and yogurt
3. Add almond butter and honey
4. Top with ice cubes
5. Blend until smooth, about 60-90 seconds
6. Pour into glasses and serve immediately

### Nutrition Highlights
- High in vitamin C from pineapple and spinach
- Potassium from banana supports heart health
- Spinach provides iron and vitamin K
- Greek yogurt adds probiotics and protein

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 2 smoothies. Best enjoyed immediately or refrigerated for up to 24 hours.

## Berry Antioxidant Blast

A powerful smoothie loaded with antioxidant-rich berries.

### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup fresh blueberries
- 1/2 cup fresh strawberries (or raspberries)
- 1/2 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1/2 cup almond milk
- 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed
- 1 tablespoon raw honey
- 1/4 cup ice

### Instructions
1. Pour almond milk into blender
2. Add blueberries and strawberries
3. Add frozen banana and yogurt
4. Sprinkle flaxseed and add honey
5. Add ice cubes
6. Blend until smooth and creamy
7. Serve in tall glasses

### Nutrition Highlights
- Berries are packed with anthocyanins and antioxidants
- Supports brain health and cognitive function
- Flaxseed adds omega-3 fatty acids
- Great source of vitamin C

### Variations
- Add 1 tablespoon peanut butter for extra protein
- Use coconut yogurt for dairy-free option
- Add spinach for extra greens

## Tropical Mango Smoothie

A refreshing tropical smoothie with mango and coconut.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh mango chunks (or frozen)
- 1/2 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup orange juice (fresh squeezed preferred)
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup ice
- Pinch of turmeric (optional)
- 1 tablespoon honey

### Instructions
1. Pour orange juice and coconut milk into blender
2. Add mango and frozen banana
3. Add yogurt and optional turmeric
4. Add honey
5. Top with ice
6. Blend until smooth and tropical
7. Pour and enjoy

### Nutrition Highlights
- Mango provides beta-carotene and vitamin A
- Orange juice adds vitamin C and citric acid
- Turmeric provides anti-inflammatory benefits
- Tropical flavors make it feel like vacation

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 2. Great as a post-workout drink or tropical breakfast.

## Carrot-Orange Immune Booster

A vibrant smoothie focused on immune system support.

### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup fresh carrot juice (or 2 medium carrots, juiced)
- 1 fresh orange, peeled and segmented
- 1/2 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup coconut yogurt
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon raw honey
- Pinch of ginger powder
- 1/4 cup ice

### Instructions
1. Add carrot juice to blender
2. Peel orange and add segments
3. Add frozen banana
4. Pour in coconut yogurt and milk
5. Add honey and ginger powder
6. Top with ice
7. Blend until creamy and smooth
8. Strain through fine mesh if desired for smoother texture

### Nutrition Highlights
- Carrots provide beta-carotene for eye and immune health
- Orange adds vitamin C for immune support
- Ginger provides anti-inflammatory compounds
- Perfect for cold prevention

### Health Benefits
- Supports immune system with high vitamin C
- Anti-inflammatory from ginger
- Great for recovery and overall wellness

## Berry & Leafy Green Detox Smoothie

A cleansing smoothie combining greens with berry sweetness.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh kale or Swiss chard, stems removed
- 1/2 cup fresh raspberries (or blackberries)
- 1/2 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
- 1/2 cup coconut water
- 1 tablespoon almond butter
- 1 tablespoon raw honey (or agave nectar)
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1/4 cup ice

### Instructions
1. Add almond milk and coconut water to blender
2. Add kale or chard
3. Blend until greens are broken down
4. Add raspberries and banana
5. Add almond butter, honey, and lemon juice
6. Add ice and blend until smooth
7. Pour immediately and serve

### Nutrition Highlights
- Kale provides vitamin K and calcium for bone health
- Raspberries add fiber and antioxidants
- Lemon aids digestion and liver function
- Coconut water provides electrolytes

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 2. Perfect for morning detox or post-workout recovery.

## Strawberry Banana Protein Smoothie

A protein-rich smoothie perfect for post-workout recovery.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh strawberries
- 1 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt (or protein powder)
- 1/2 cup milk of choice
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed
- 1/4 cup ice
- Pinch of vanilla extract

### Instructions
1. Add milk to blender first
2. Add fresh strawberries
3. Add frozen banana and Greek yogurt
4. Add honey and flaxseed
5. Add vanilla extract
6. Top with ice
7. Blend until creamy and thick
8. Pour into tall glass

### Nutrition Highlights
- High in protein from Greek yogurt
- Strawberries provide vitamin C and manganese
- Banana provides potassium for muscle recovery
- Great for post-exercise nutrition

### Variations
- Use plant-based yogurt for vegan option
- Add 1 scoop vanilla protein powder for extra protein
- Use strawberry jam for sweeter version

## Tips for Perfect Smoothies

### Best Practices
- **Frozen fruit** creates creamier texture; freeze ripe bananas with peel
- **Order matters**: Layer liquids first, frozen items, then powders
- **Sweetness**: Start with 1 tablespoon honey, add more to taste
- **Texture**: Blend 60-90 seconds for smooth; blend less for chunkier texture
- **Storage**: Best enjoyed immediately; refrigerate up to 24 hours

### Ingredient Substitutions
- **Milk options**: Almond, coconut, oat, rice, or regular dairy milk
- **Yogurt**: Greek, regular, coconut, or cashew-based
- **Sweeteners**: Honey, agave, maple syrup, or dates
- **Proteins**: Greek yogurt, protein powder, nut butters, or tofu
