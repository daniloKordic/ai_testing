# Tomatoes

## Overview
Tomatoes are fruits (botanically speaking) commonly used as vegetables in culinary applications. They belong to the nightshade family and are beloved for their juicy texture, tangy-sweet flavor, and versatility in cuisines worldwide.

## Varieties
- **Beefsteak**: Large, meaty tomatoes ideal for slicing
- **Cherry**: Small, sweet tomatoes perfect for snacking and salads
- **Roma (Plum)**: Oblong, firm tomatoes ideal for sauces and canning
- **Heirloom**: Diverse colors and flavors, often more complex taste
- **Grape**: Small, elongated tomatoes with concentrated sweetness
- **Green**: Unripe tomatoes with tart, crisp flavor

## Ripeness Stages
- **Green**: Unripe, firm, tart flavor; used for fried green tomatoes
- **Pink/Turning**: Beginning to ripen, mild flavor
- **Full Red**: Fully ripe, sweet, juicy, optimal flavor and nutrition

## Nutritional Profile
- Rich in lycopene, a powerful antioxidant
- Good source of vitamin C and vitamin K
- Contains potassium for heart health
- Rich in folate and chromium
- About 18 calories per 100g
- Contains naringenin, a flavonoid with anti-inflammatory properties
- Beta-carotene content increases with ripeness

## Health Benefits
- Lycopene may reduce risk of heart disease and certain cancers
- Supports heart health with potassium and folate
- Promotes bone health with vitamin K
- Supports eye health and vision
- May help regulate blood pressure
- Contains compounds that support prostate health
- Anti-inflammatory properties
- May improve skin health and protect against sun damage

## Growing Information
- **Climate**: Warm season crop; thrive in 70-85°F (21-29°C) temperatures
- **Soil**: Well-drained, fertile soil with good organic matter
- **Water**: Consistent moisture; 1-2 inches per week; avoid wetting leaves
- **Sunlight**: Full sun (6-8+ hours daily)
- **Support**: Most varieties need stakes or cages
- **Harvest Time**: 60-85 days; harvest when fully colored but still slightly soft

## Storage
- Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight
- Do not refrigerate unless fully ripe (damages flavor and texture)
- Keep stem-side down to slow ripening
- Store away from ethylene-producing fruits
- Ripe tomatoes keep 3-5 days at room temperature
- Can be frozen whole for later use in sauces

## Culinary Uses
- Eaten fresh in salads and sandwiches
- Used in salsas and ceviche
- Made into sauces and purees for pasta
- Roasted with herbs and olive oil
- Used in soups and bisques
- Grilled as a side dish
- Canned and preserved for winter use
- Incorporated into curries and stews
- Juiced for fresh tomato juice
- Used in gazpacho and cold soups
