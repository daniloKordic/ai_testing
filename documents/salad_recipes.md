# Fresh & Vibrant Salad Recipes

## Mediterranean Tomato & Pepper Salad

A colorful summer salad combining fresh tomatoes, peppers, and leafy greens.

### Ingredients
- 4 medium ripe tomatoes, diced
- 2 bell peppers (red and yellow), diced
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 4 cups mixed leafy greens (arugula, spinach, lettuce)
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1/4 cup Kalamata olives
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Prepare all vegetables and wash leafy greens
2. In large bowl, combine diced tomatoes, peppers, cucumber
3. Add red onion and fresh basil
4. In separate bowl, whisk olive oil and vinegar with salt and pepper
5. Pour dressing over vegetables and toss gently
6. Add mixed greens to bowl and toss again
7. Top with feta cheese and olives
8. Serve immediately or chill for up to 2 hours

### Nutrition Highlights
- Lycopene from tomatoes supports heart health
- Vitamin C from peppers aids immune function
- Leafy greens provide iron and vitamin K
- Olive oil provides healthy monounsaturated fats

### Variations
- Add grilled chicken for protein
- Include artichoke hearts or sun-dried tomatoes
- Add chickpeas for plant-based protein

## Rainbow Carrot & Beet Salad

A colorful salad celebrating root vegetables and greens.

### Ingredients
- 2 medium carrots, julienned or thinly sliced
- 1 medium beet, julienned (raw or roasted)
- 4 cups mixed salad greens (spinach, arugula, lettuce)
- 1 apple, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup raw almonds, sliced
- 1/4 cup dried cranberries
- 2 tablespoons pumpkin seeds
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon honey
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Prepare carrots and beets; if using raw beets, use gloves to avoid staining
2. Arrange mixed greens as base
3. Layer carrots and beets on greens
4. Top with apple slices, almonds, and cranberries
5. In small bowl, whisk olive oil, vinegar, mustard, and honey
6. Drizzle dressing over salad
7. Sprinkle pumpkin seeds on top
8. Serve immediately

### Nutrition Highlights
- Carrots provide beta-carotene for eye health
- Beets contain betalains with anti-inflammatory properties
- Nuts add healthy fats and protein
- Apple adds fiber and natural sweetness

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4 as a side or 2 as a main course. Great for lunch prep.

## Kale Caesar Salad with Homemade Dressing

A modern twist on a classic, using nutrient-dense kale.

### Ingredients
- 6 cups fresh kale, stems removed and chopped
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 1/2 cup whole wheat croutons
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1/4 teaspoon anchovy paste (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Lemon wedges for serving

### Instructions
1. In large bowl, massage kale with 1 tablespoon olive oil for 1-2 minutes until softened
2. In small bowl, whisk remaining olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, mustard, anchovy paste, and Worcestershire
3. Pour dressing over kale and toss to coat evenly
4. Add Parmesan cheese and mix gently
5. Top with croutons
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Serve with lemon wedges

### Nutrition Highlights
- Kale is a nutritional powerhouse with vitamin K, calcium, and iron
- Massage helps break down tough fibers and makes greens more digestible
- Lemon juice aids calcium absorption
- Parmesan adds flavor with minimal calories

### Variations
- Add grilled chicken or chickpeas for protein
- Include roasted tomatoes or carrots
- Use vegan Parmesan for dairy-free option

## Strawberry Spinach Salad with Poppy Seed Dressing

A sweet and savory salad combining berries with nutritious greens.

### Ingredients
- 6 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup fresh strawberries, sliced
- 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese (or feta)
- 1/4 cup sliced almonds, toasted
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 tablespoon poppy seeds
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Wash and dry spinach; place in large bowl
2. Add sliced strawberries, onion, and almonds
3. In small bowl, whisk olive oil, vinegar, honey, Dijon mustard, and poppy seeds
4. Pour dressing over salad and toss gently
5. Top with crumbled goat cheese
6. Serve immediately

### Nutrition Highlights
- Spinach provides iron and folate
- Strawberries offer vitamin C and antioxidants
- Almonds add protein and healthy fats
- Goat cheese provides calcium and probiotics

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Perfect as a light lunch or dinner starter.

## Asian Broccoli Slaw with Sesame Dressing

A crunchy, refreshing salad with Asian-inspired flavors.

### Ingredients
- 4 cups fresh broccoli florets, thinly sliced
- 2 medium carrots, julienned
- 1/2 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 cups purple cabbage, shredded
- 1/4 cup green onions, sliced
- 1/4 cup sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup sliced almonds

### Dressing
- 3 tablespoons sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Pinch of red pepper flakes

### Instructions
1. Slice broccoli florets thinly using knife or mandoline
2. In large bowl, combine broccoli, carrots, bell pepper, and cabbage
3. In small bowl, whisk all dressing ingredients until combined
4. Pour dressing over vegetables and toss well
5. Let sit for 15-30 minutes to allow flavors to meld
6. Top with sesame seeds and almonds before serving
7. Add green onions just before serving

### Nutrition Highlights
- Broccoli provides sulforaphane with anti-cancer properties
- Carrots add beta-carotene for eye health
- Sesame seeds provide calcium and healthy fats
- Ginger aids digestion and reduces inflammation

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4-6. Great as a side dish or light main with grilled tofu or chicken.

## Roasted Vegetable & Berry Salad

A warm salad combining roasted vegetables with fresh berries.

### Ingredients
- 2 bell peppers, cut into chunks
- 2 medium carrots, cut into rounds
- 1 small head broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 cups mixed greens
- 1 cup fresh strawberries or raspberries
- 1/4 cup candied pecans or walnuts
- 1/4 cup crumbled goat cheese

### Vinaigrette
- 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon honey

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss peppers, carrots, and broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper
3. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through
4. Cool roasted vegetables to room temperature
5. Whisk vinaigrette ingredients in small bowl
6. Arrange mixed greens on plate
7. Top with cooled roasted vegetables and fresh berries
8. Drizzle with vinaigrette
9. Garnish with candied nuts and goat cheese

### Nutrition Highlights
- Roasting concentrates vegetable flavors and nutrients
- Peppers and carrots provide vitamins A and C
- Berries add sweetness and antioxidants
- Nuts provide healthy fats and crunch

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Great as a vegetarian main course or side with protein.
