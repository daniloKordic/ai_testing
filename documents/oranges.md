# Oranges

## Overview
Oranges are citrus fruits known for their bright orange color and sweet-tart flavor. They are one of the most widely cultivated fruit trees in the world.

## Varieties
- **Valencia Orange**: The most common sweet orange, often used for juice
- **Navel Orange**: Large seedless oranges with a distinctive navel at one end
- **Blood Orange**: Deep red flesh with a slightly tart flavor
- **Seville Orange**: Bitter orange used primarily for marmalade and cooking

## Nutritional Benefits
- High in vitamin C, which supports immune function
- Contains fiber for digestive health
- Good source of folate and potassium
- Rich in antioxidants and flavonoids
- Low in calories (about 47 calories per 100g)

## Health Benefits
- Boosts immune system
- Promotes heart health
- Aids in iron absorption due to high vitamin C content
- May reduce inflammation
- Supports bone health with calcium and vitamin D in fortified varieties

## Growing Information
- **Climate**: Thrive in warm, subtropical, and tropical climates
- **Soil**: Prefer well-drained soil
- **Water**: Moderate watering required; need protection from waterlogging
- **Harvest**: Typically ripen in winter (November to March in Northern Hemisphere)

## Storage
- Store at room temperature for 2-3 weeks
- Refrigerate in a plastic bag for up to 2 months
- Oranges continue to release ethylene gas, affecting other produce nearby

## Culinary Uses
- Fresh juice and smoothies
- Desserts and baked goods
- Marmalade and preserves
- Glazes for meats and vegetables
- Aromatics in tea and cocktails
