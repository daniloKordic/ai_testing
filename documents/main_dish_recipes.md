# Main Dish Recipes

## Roasted Tomato & Pepper Pasta

A light, flavorful pasta dish celebrating fresh garden vegetables.

### Ingredients
- 6 medium tomatoes, halved
- 2 large bell peppers (red and yellow), cut into chunks
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 12 oz pasta (penne or rigatoni)
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)
2. Place tomatoes and peppers on baking sheet
3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper
4. Scatter minced garlic over vegetables
5. Roast for 25-30 minutes until vegetables are soft and slightly charred
6. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions
7. Transfer roasted vegetables to blender or food processor
8. Add fresh basil and pulse until sauce reaches desired consistency
9. Toss hot pasta with roasted vegetable sauce
10. Top with Parmesan cheese and red pepper flakes

### Nutrition Highlights
- Lycopene from tomatoes supports heart health
- Vitamin C from peppers aids immune function
- Basil provides antioxidants and anti-inflammatory compounds
- Whole grain pasta option adds fiber

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Pairs well with a green salad or crusty bread.

## Stir-Fried Broccoli & Carrots with Ginger

A quick, healthy vegetable stir-fry with Asian flavors.

### Ingredients
- 4 cups fresh broccoli florets
- 3 medium carrots, sliced into thin rounds
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Cooked rice for serving

### Instructions
1. Prepare all vegetables and have them ready before cooking
2. Heat sesame oil in large wok or skillet over high heat
3. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 30 seconds until fragrant
4. Add broccoli florets and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes
5. Add sliced carrots and continue cooking for 3-4 minutes
6. In small bowl, combine soy sauce, vinegar, honey, and red pepper flakes
7. Pour sauce into wok and toss vegetables to coat
8. Cook for 1-2 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp
9. Top with sesame seeds and green onions
10. Serve over steamed rice

### Nutrition Highlights
- Broccoli provides sulforaphane with anti-cancer properties
- Carrots rich in beta-carotene for eye health
- Ginger aids digestion and reduces inflammation
- Low in calories, high in nutrients

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Great as a vegetarian main or side with grilled protein.

## Stuffed Bell Peppers with Quinoa & Vegetables

A hearty, protein-rich vegetarian main dish.

### Ingredients
- 4 large bell peppers (various colors), halved and seeds removed
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 2 medium carrots, finely diced
- 1 cup fresh broccoli, finely chopped
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1/2 cup vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Place pepper halves in baking dish
3. Heat olive oil in large skillet over medium heat
4. Sauté onion and garlic for 2-3 minutes
5. Add carrots and broccoli, cook for 5 minutes
6. Stir in cooked quinoa, diced tomatoes, and broth
7. Add Italian herbs, salt, and pepper
8. Simmer for 5 minutes to blend flavors
9. Fold in feta cheese
10. Divide mixture among pepper halves
11. Cover with foil and bake for 25-30 minutes
12. Remove foil and bake additional 5 minutes

### Nutrition Highlights
- Quinoa provides complete protein with all amino acids
- Bell peppers provide vitamin C and antioxidants
- Broccoli and carrots add vitamins and minerals
- Feta cheese provides calcium and probiotics

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Complete meal that can be made ahead and reheated.

## Tomato & Kale Pasta with Garlic Oil

A simple, elegant pasta dish highlighting fresh produce.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz pasta (any shape)
- 6 cups fresh kale, stems removed and chopped
- 4 medium tomatoes, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup vegetable broth
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. Cook pasta according to package directions; drain and reserve 1 cup pasta water
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in large skillet over medium-low heat
3. Add sliced garlic and red pepper flakes; cook for 2-3 minutes until fragrant
4. Increase heat to medium and add chopped kale
5. Stir frequently for 3-4 minutes until kale begins to soften
6. Add diced tomatoes and vegetable broth
7. Simmer for 5 minutes to blend flavors
8. Add cooked pasta to skillet and toss to combine
9. Add pasta water as needed to create sauce
10. Finish with lemon juice and season with salt and pepper
11. Top with Parmesan cheese

### Nutrition Highlights
- Kale provides vitamins K, A, and calcium
- Tomatoes offer lycopene and vitamin C
- Garlic provides sulfur compounds with anti-inflammatory properties
- Simple preparation preserves nutrient content

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Light and healthy main course ready in 20 minutes.

## Roasted Carrot & Orange Soup

A vibrant, creamy soup celebrating root vegetables and citrus.

### Ingredients
- 4 medium carrots, cut into chunks
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup fresh orange juice
- Zest of 1 orange
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 cup coconut milk or heavy cream
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon ginger powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional)

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C)
2. Toss carrot chunks with 1 tablespoon olive oil, salt, and pepper
3. Roast for 20 minutes until tender
4. Heat remaining olive oil in large pot over medium heat
5. Sauté onion and garlic for 3-4 minutes
6. Add roasted carrots to pot with vegetable broth
7. Bring to simmer and cook for 5 minutes
8. Using immersion blender, blend soup until smooth
9. Stir in orange juice, orange zest, coconut milk, cumin, and ginger
10. Heat through but do not boil
11. Season with salt and pepper
12. Garnish with cilantro if desired

### Nutrition Highlights
- Carrots provide beta-carotene for eye and immune health
- Orange juice adds vitamin C and aids absorption of beta-carotene
- Warming spices aid digestion
- Low in calories, high in nutrients

### Serving Suggestion
Serves 4. Perfect autumn or winter soup. Can be made ahead and reheated.

## Pepper-Based Vegetable Curry

A warming curry with peppers, tomatoes, and aromatic spices.

### Ingredients
- 2 bell peppers (red and yellow), cut into chunks
- 4 medium tomatoes, diced
- 2 jalapeños, seeds removed and minced
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Cooked rice for serving

### Instructions
1. Heat oil in large pot over medium heat
2. Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, and jalapeños for 3-4 minutes
3. Add curry powder, cumin, coriander, and turmeric; cook for 1 minute
4. Add bell peppers and tomatoes; stir well
5. Pour in coconut milk and vegetable broth
6. Bring to simmer and cook for 15-20 minutes
7. Vegetables should be tender but not mushy
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Garnish with fresh cilantro
10. Serve over steamed rice

### Nutrition Highlights
- Peppers provide vitamins C and A
- Tomatoes offer lycopene and antioxidants
- Curry spices have anti-inflammatory properties
- Coconut milk provides healthy fats
- Turmeric supports brain health and reduces inflammation

### Variations
- Add chickpeas for protein
- Include carrots and broccoli
- Use spinach or kale for additional greens
